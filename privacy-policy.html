Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Chess Arena ("we," "us," "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and any profile information you provide, via Supabase Authentication. We also store gameplay data such as puzzle progress and in-app coin balances to operate the app's features.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads, including rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data to serve relevant ads and measure performance, as governed by Google's own privacy policy and advertising guidelines.

How we use your information

We use collected data to operate your account, save your progress, display ads, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase's infrastructure. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us (see below). Deleting your account will permanently remove your puzzle progress and coin balance.

Children's privacy

Chess Arena is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
