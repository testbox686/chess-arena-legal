Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Chess Arena ("we," "us," "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and any profile information you provide, via Supabase Authentication. We also store gameplay data such as puzzle progress and in-app coin balances to operate the app's features.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads, including rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant advertisements.
